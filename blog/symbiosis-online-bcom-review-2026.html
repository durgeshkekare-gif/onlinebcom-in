August 05, 2026 · 6 min read · onlinebcom.in

Symbiosis Online BCom — Overview

Symbiosis Online BCom is offered by SSODL (Symbiosis School for Online and Digital Learning), the online arm of Symbiosis International University. NAAC A++ accredited — India's highest accreditation grade. WES recognised. Total fee approximately ₹1.07L (₹54K per year × 2 years + exam fees). 3-year programme.

Symbiosis BCom — Curriculum

  • Year 1: Financial Accounting, Business Economics, Business Mathematics, Business Communication
  • Year 2: Cost Accounting, Corporate Accounting, Business Law, Income Tax
  • Year 3: Auditing, Financial Management, Strategic Management, Elective (Finance/Marketing/HR)

Symbiosis BCom — Honest Assessment

Strengths: NAAC A++ gives the highest accreditation grade. WES recognition for international use. Symbiosis is a well-regarded brand particularly in Pune and West India. Fee (₹1.07L) is affordable for the accreditation level — cheaper than Amrita.

Limitations: No industry certifications bundled (unlike Amrita's Oracle/Amazon or Manipal's Google Cloud). Placement support is limited — outcomes depend on self-initiative. Symbiosis brand is stronger in Pune region than nationally.

"Symbiosis BCom is the best value for NAAC A++ accreditation — strongest grade at mid-range fee. Choose it over Manipal if accreditation grade matters more than brand name."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Symbiosis Online BCom valid?

Yes — Symbiosis Online BCom is UGC-DEB approved, from NAAC A++ accredited Symbiosis International University, and WES recognised. It is a valid BCom degree for employment, higher studies and immigration purposes.

Symbiosis vs IGNOU for online BCom — which is better?

IGNOU BCom (₹20K) is from a central government statutory university — maximum affordability and government recognition. Symbiosis BCom (₹1.07L) has NAAC A++, WES recognition and stronger employer brand in private sector. For government job applications: IGNOU may have an advantage. For private sector and international use: Symbiosis is clearly superior.

What is the placement support for Symbiosis Online BCom?

Symbiosis SSODL provides access to the placement portal and career services. However, placements for online programmes are not as structured as the residential campus. Students should actively use the alumni network, apply through job portals, and supplement with certifications for better outcomes.
