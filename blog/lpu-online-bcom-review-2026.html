LPU Online BCom — Overview

LPU Online BCom (Bachelor of Commerce) is offered through LPU Distance Education (LPUDE), the online arm of Lovely Professional University (LPU), Punjab. NAAC A+ accredited. WES recognised. Fee approximately ₹80K total over 3 years. One of the most affordable online BCom options from a NAAC A+ university.

LPU Online BCom — Strengths

  • NAAC A+ — strong accreditation grade
  • WES recognised — important for Canada PR applications
  • One of the most affordable online BCom from a NAAC A+ university (~₹80K vs ₹1.35L for Amrita)
  • LPU brand — large campus in Jalandhar, growing national recognition
  • Large placement drives — LPU has one of the largest on-campus placement ecosystems in India
  • Active LPU alumni network — 2 lakh+ alumni globally

LPU Online BCom — Limitations

  • NAAC A+ vs Amrita/Symbiosis A++ — accreditation grade is one level lower
  • No industry certifications bundled (unlike Amrita's Oracle/Amazon bundle)
  • LPU brand primarily strong in North India — less recognized in South India corporate circles
  • Placement support is better for residential students; online students have portal access but not equal to campus experience

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LPU Online BCom valid?

Yes — LPU Online BCom is UGC-DEB approved, NAAC A+ accredited, and WES recognised. It is a valid Bachelor of Commerce degree for employment, higher studies, and immigration purposes.

LPU vs Manipal for Online BCom — which is better?

Both are NAAC A+, WES recognised, and fee approximately ₹80K. Manipal MUJ has marginally stronger national brand recognition and Google Cloud certification bundled. LPU has stronger brand in North India (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Delhi NCR) and larger alumni network volume. Choose Manipal for national use; choose LPU if North India network is your primary target.

What is the placement support for LPU Online BCom?

LPU provides access to its placement portal, career fairs (online), and alumni network for online students. LPU's physical campus has one of India's largest placement drives — some of this infrastructure extends to online students. However, physical campus students have significantly better placement access than online students at most universities including LPU.
